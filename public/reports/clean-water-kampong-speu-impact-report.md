# Clean Water for Kampong Speu — Impact Report

## Summary

JCI Phnom Penh partnered with local community leaders to install 6 community
water filtration units across Kampong Speu province, bringing safe drinking
water to an estimated 1,200 residents across 4 villages.

## Key Results

- **6** community water filters installed
- **4** villages served
- **1,200** residents with improved access to clean drinking water
- **12** JCI Phnom Penh volunteers contributed install-day labor

# JCI Phnom Penh — Media Kit

## About JCI Phnom Penh

JCI Phnom Penh is the local chapter of Junior Chamber International (JCI) in
Phnom Penh, Cambodia — a global network of young active citizens aged 18–40
working on community development, business development, and leadership
training. Members run community projects, host general membership meetings
(GMMs), training workshops, and fundraising events throughout the year.

**Mission:** To provide development opportunities that empower young people
to create positive change.

**Vision:** To be the leading global network of young active citizens.

## Logo Usage

- Maintain clear space around the logo equal to the height of the "JCI" mark.
- Do not recolor, stretch, or add effects (shadows, outlines) to the logo.
- On dark backgrounds, use the white/gold lockup; on light backgrounds, use
  the navy lockup.
- Logo files: contact the chapter press officer (below) for current
  high-resolution assets — this kit ships with brand guidelines only.

## Press Contact

For interview requests, event coverage, or partnership inquiries, reach the
chapter via the **Sponsor a Project** or membership contact forms on
jcphnompenh.org, and a board member will respond within one week.
